# EssayDesk — Descriptive Exam Essay Practice Studio

A clean, distraction-free web-based essay writing studio built for descriptive exam preparation (UPSC CSE, SEBI Grade A, IBPS PO/SO, RBI Grade B, and other competitive exams).

🔗 **Live App:** [https://dinesh1115.github.io/wordcount-essay-studio/](https://dinesh1115.github.io/wordcount-essay-studio/) *(or `/exam_essay_practice_studio.html`)*

---

##  Features

- **Distraction-Free Paper Layout:** Editable exam topic header with built-in sample prompts, `Tab`-key paragraph indentation, Serif/Sans-serif font toggle, and a **Focus Mode** that hides extra toolbars while writing.
- **Real-Time Word & Text Metrics:** Live tracking for word count, character count (with and without spaces), sentence count, paragraph count, and estimated reading time.
- **Exam Word Limit Tracker:** Quick presets (`150`, `200`, `250`, `500`, `1000` words) plus custom targets with a color-coded progress bar that turns green inside the ideal **90%–105%** exam window and red if you exceed the limit.
- **Exam Countdown Timer & Stopwatch:** Presets (`15`, `20`, `30`, `60` minutes) and custom durations with Start/Pause/Reset controls, low-time visual alert under 1 minute, and a Stopwatch mode.
- **Auto-Save & Export:** Automatically saves drafts, topic prompts, target limits, and Dark/Light theme settings in `localStorage`, with one-click Copy and `.txt` file export.

---

## How to Use

1. Open the live link in any desktop or mobile browser.
2. Enter your essay topic (or pick one from the sample prompts).
3. Set your **Target Word Count** and **Timer** to match your exam pattern.
4. Click **Start** and write—your progress saves automatically in your browser.
5. Export your essay as a `.txt` file or copy it when finished.
